A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Clarity before commitment.

Do consultation credits have to be used monthly?+

No. Guided consultations are intended to be flexible. You can use several early in your program, space them out, or combine them for a longer meeting.

Does the meal plan include groceries?+

Your plan includes meal and grocery planning tools; ingredients, retailer delivery fees, and taxes are paid separately. Retailer checkout integrations are planned, not live yet.

Can I meet my dietitian if I only buy the 28-day menu?+

Yes. The digital program includes the option to schedule one complimentary 15-minute introduction, with no obligation to upgrade.
